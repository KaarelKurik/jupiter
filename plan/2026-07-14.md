# 2026-07-14 — step 4 probed before built; the blend fork widened into a representation fork

- **Method decision up front**: measure before implementing. Four disposable
  probe rounds (scratchpad) against the AD christoffel as truth; findings in
  measurements.md 2026-07-14. A recording env riding the (previously unused)
  env slot instrumented the physics_diff bundles — the env slot is confirmed
  as the clean dispatch extension point for a future `christoffel(::ΓTables, v)`.
- **What the probes established** (condensed; measurements.md has the data):
  - Stage excursions are large (trefoil: 19% of Γ evals beyond the settled
    quadrant at h=0.05; rays dip below d=0) — tables need margins + AD
    fallback.
  - Depth factors out of tabulation *exactly*: g_outer quadratic in d
    (collar structure), g_inner d-independent, depth blend + derivative
    evaluated exactly at runtime. Tables are 24 lateral 2D fields per wedge
    (A, B, C, im), derivatives by Chebyshev coefficient recurrence — no
    derivative storage.
  - Lateral domain: seam-aligned per-wedge polar sectors + tiny Cartesian
    core (1/r derivative blowup at the center otherwise). Naive boxes that
    straddle seams barely converge. Partition of unity dies past the face
    ring, so box validity is a hard constraint.
  - Accuracy is corner-blend-limited, with complementary failure modes:
    exp-flat plateaus ~1e-3 relative Γ (bad seams, good vertices); C⁴ nonic
    reaches ~1e-5 and falling (good seams, genuinely singular at irregular
    vertices — fractional power through z^(n/4); fallback disc covers it);
    C² is worse than both (Γ needs ∂g). Trefoil (valence-4-only) is easy
    for everything — foreshadowing the affine-atlas story below.
  - AD christoffel measured 26–32 µs/eval; table eval estimated 1–4 µs —
    the roadmap's 10–50x per step stands.
  - A false negative en route: round 2's "anchor" spanned the full wedge
    angle and failed at 0.75; the fixed interior anchor collapses to ~1e-12,
    validating the pipeline. Sup-error sampling noise ±2x at M=1500.
- **Kaarel's read**: irregular-vertex + blend issues "kind of grim"; would
  take C⁴ *if* staying on this approach, but wants the broader redesign
  considered — manifold splines (he recalled the single-extraordinary-point
  constructions), and ideally level-set, where his past searches never found
  a rep with all the virtues (cheap eval/precompute, locality, topology
  respect). New idea from him: superfrusta (arXiv 2512.09201) + metaballs.
- **Mapping established in discussion**: manifold-spline "extraordinary"
  (affine structure obstructed) = valence ≠ 4 = exactly our bad tabulation
  cores; trefoil = torus (χ=0) = zero extraordinary points = the case our
  probes already showed converging everywhere. Affine transitions make
  corner blending unnecessary outright — that, not EP count, is the prize.
  EP count vs severity is a design axis: single-EP (Ricci flow) concentrates
  4π in one savage point; 8 mild cones + caps (TURBS/guided splines) likely
  kinder.
- **Recorded**: all paths consolidated in plan/claude.md "Representation
  fork (recorded 2026-07-14)" — C⁴-on-YZ, affine-atlas family, level-set
  candidates (superfrusta+metaballs with the C³-of-φ caveat, IMLS, CS-HRBF),
  plus the orthogonal step-size lever. Session deliberately left there:
  kaarel picks the path; step-4 implementation (tasks queued) resumes after.
- Session shape note (kaarel feedback): probe conclusions without the
  reasoning chain don't land — summaries must carry the *why*, and
  measurements.md now does.

# 2026-07-14, second session — the simpler road: production christoffel straight onto the card; Float32 makes it real

- **Kaarel's direction**: before the representation fork / tabulation, try
  the simple thing — run the AD christoffels directly on the GPU and see;
  limit pre-port optimization. This session is that measurement.
- **Enabler, not optimization**: the type layer went storage-parametric
  (Mesh/Surface/Throat/HalfThroat/Chart/SituatedPhase/AmbientRay + Scene,
  SituatedCamera, TessellatedMouth, CatmullClarkRes). Pure type
  generalization — 275 tests + physics_diff bit-identical — but it lets
  Adapt carry the *real* Throat into a kernel (ragged construction-only
  Mesh fields → nothing; packed_polys → one padded 4D array behind a view,
  padding at the leading end of the descending Horner loop so eval is
  bit-identical). The kernel builds the tracer's own SituatedPhase and
  calls production christoffel unmodified; no physics was duplicated.
  (One honest casualty: parametric structs stop auto-converting field
  types, which flushed out an untyped Dict{Any,Any} in catmullclark.)
- **Float64 answer: compiles, loses.** No second-order-AD compilability
  wall (the open step-3 question). But 48-Float64 nested-dual scalars blow
  the 255-register cap into 52KB/thread of local spill on a 1:64-FP64
  card: 3.9 µs/eval mixed = 5.5x one CPU thread ≈ 1/3 of the 16-thread
  CPU. Naive F64 direct-AD is not a port.
- **Float32 answer: the road.** After an eltype-honesty sweep of the
  christoffel path (carrier(x) = innermost primal type in ad.jl; the
  sqrt2/2π-sincos/wedge-power/0.5 literals and ThroatParams reads convert
  instead of promoting — F64 certified bit-identical since the conversions
  are identity there): 1.43 µs/eval mixed = 14.3x one thread ≈ the whole
  CPU, *naive*; divergence datum says depth-binning recovers ~2x (~29x);
  spill halved but still spill-bound, so this is a floor. Accuracy vs F64
  truth: sup-rel median 9.5e-7 / p99 3.8e-6 / max 1.1e-5 — **better than
  the C⁴ table target, 100x better than exp-flat, at exact blend
  semantics** (nothing re-fit, re-baselined, re-rendered).
- **Consequence recorded in the plan**: the representation fork no longer
  gates the GPU port — the card can afford to evaluate the truth. Fork
  stays open as the CPU-perf/elegance question. Step 4 demoted from the
  GPU critical path.
- **What this does not answer** (flagged in measurements.md 2026-07-14b):
  Γ-eval error ≠ exit-map error. F32 positions accumulate over hundreds of
  steps and chaotic bands amplify; the F32+F64-fallback pixel policy needs
  an end-to-end traced comparison, which first needs the eltype sweep
  extended to steppers/settle/transitions. That plus the wavefront
  restructure is the remaining step-5 work; register-pressure surgery on
  the fused dual passes deliberately not done today.
- Method note, per the 07-14a feedback: measurements.md 2026-07-14b
  carries the full why-chain (spill arithmetic, divergence decomposition,
  what the accuracy numbers are relative to).
- **Kaarel, post-measurement** (recorded in plan step 5): (1) further
  optimization lean — hand-roll the derivatives in production, keep AD in
  reference only; needed down the line anyway for portability beyond
  Nvidia (and it attacks the measured bound directly: the spill is dual
  bloat, and Γ's derivative inventory — s..∂³s through poly∘wedge∘blend —
  is closed-form throughout; certification is the existing
  reference-as-oracle contract, one layer down). (2) Correction on my
  proposed F32 acceptance metric: sup exit-map error is vacuous — the exit
  map image→(side, ray) is discontinuous (side variation at the
  limit-cycle boundary), so worst-case error is unbounded a priori for any
  finite-precision method; judge F32 by the physics_diff decomposition
  (side-flip rate = undecidable-band size, deviation conditional on
  agreement) plus eyeballed render pairs.
- **Conditional-deviation statistics** (kaarel probing the metric further):
  conditional-on-agreement deviation is still power-law-tailed (deviation ~
  ε·κ(ray), κ ~ r^(−λ/λ′) from log-divergent winding time near the
  limit-cycle boundary), so not only max but the *mean* can fail to exist —
  sample means would be dominated by the closest-to-boundary draw. Safe
  aggregates: quantiles, log-mean, exceedance fraction at a sub-pixel
  angular threshold (with side-flip rate: "fraction of pixels wrong for
  precision reasons"). The method/scene factorization is the principled
  frame: ε is the method, κ the scene; isolate ε by quantile-curve
  parallelism vs an F64 1-ulp-perturbation baseline, or normalize per-ray
  by Jacobi-field κ — same machinery as the planned adaptive ray bundles
  and the F64-fallback pixel flag. Passage-count binning is the cheap
  proxy ordinal for distance-to-boundary until then. Recorded in plan
  step 5.

# 2026-07-14, third session — eltype-honest tracer; F32 judged end-to-end and accepted

- **Continuation of step 5** per the 14b reorder: the eltype sweep extended
  past christoffel so F32 traces exist, then the traced F32-vs-F64
  comparison under kaarel's decomposition metric (not sup exit-map error).
- **The sweep** (src commit, F64 certified bit-identical — 275 tests +
  physics_diff exactly 0.0): dopri tableau ratios, tracer h/tol/floatmin,
  RK4 h, half_transition's transition_depth read, to_ambient's Placement
  reads, emit_ray's 1.0, view_phase_at_target's angle (Float64-exact, one
  rounding in — the wedge_square_coords idiom), and `directional`'s 0.0
  derivative seed in ad.jl. Everything converts to carrier(x) at the read
  site; ThroatParams/Placement stay Float64 structs by design.
- **Design of the F32 trace**: mouth entry stays an F64 Newton solve
  against the F32-coefficient surface (once per passage, well-conditioned,
  the plausible wavefront split anyway); phase converts at entry; in-throat
  trace wholly F32 on F32-rounded tables (F64 tables would silently promote
  eval_packed); exit converts at to_ambient, re-entry in F64.
- **Instrument**: scripts/precision_diff.jl, tracked deliberately (the
  standing precision-acceptance tool, physics_diff's sibling — the
  disposable-probe convention doesn't apply). 8000-ray bundles × 4
  variants: truth / 1-ulp-perturbed (noise baseline) / tab32 (F64
  arithmetic on F32 tables — isolates representation rounding) / f32.
- **Verdict — F32 accepted** (measurements.md 2026-07-14c has the full
  data): trefoil f32/ulp quantile offset flat at 28.2–28.4 log2 across
  q50–q99 (parallel = pure precision scaling, no pathology; 2^29 would be
  the naive mantissa ratio); side flips 0/8000 cube, 1/8000 + 2
  res-mismatches trefoil, all at passage ≥ 3 (passage count works as the
  cheap boundary-distance ordinal — deviation medians grow ~8x/passage);
  exceedance-at-1-mrad 0.04% cube / 1.1% trefoil; tab32 shows the tables
  alone contribute ~⅓ of the f32 median (the representation floor).
  Eyeball bar: 384×288 render pairs indistinguishable, diff structure =
  the two-image boundary filaments, flipped pixels (1 and 12 of 110592)
  sit on them — the undecidable band is the limit-cycle boundary, as
  predicted. The ulp baseline's own 1e-16→2.4e-9 amplification shows F64's
  band is thinner, not absent.
- **Remaining step-5 work**: the wavefront restructure (+ depth binning,
  ~2x); hand-rolled derivatives stay the named register-pressure lever;
  Jacobi-field κ normalization stays the endgame for the fallback flag.

# 2026-07-14, fourth session — wavefront restructure landed; the staged driver is faster on CPU too

- **The remaining step-5 blocker, done.** The passage loop's per-passage
  sum-type boxes (trace_ray's Union{Nothing, AmbientRay}, trace_geodesic's
  Union{SituatedPhase, AmbientRay}) became a stage tag on one isbits
  `WavefrontRay{T}` record: side, chart handle id, passage/step counters,
  dopri's adaptive h, pos, vel (src/wavefront.jl).
- **Kernel body**: `sweep_ray` advances one in-throat ray up to K settled
  integrator attempts in the ray's own precision, replicating both
  trace_geodesic loops op-for-op (settle → exit-check order, dopri's
  cap/accept/h-update order, exact carrier conversions); resumable across
  sweeps because h and the attempt count live in the record; 0 bytes
  allocated, both integrator modes. Measurement footnote: `@allocated` on
  non-const globals charges ~80 phantom bytes of caller dispatch — measure
  behind a function barrier (the test does).
- **Stages**: mouth entry stays the host-side F64 solve — the seam 14c
  certified; the passage budget is charged there, reproducing trace_ray's
  exits-on-last-passage shape exactly (a ray exiting its last allowed
  passage never gets another entry solve → unresolved). The initial camera
  ray never lives in a T record, so its first entry sees full F64; every
  later T→F64→T round trip is an exact embedding. Driver: pixel-parallel
  pool + active-index list, sweep → compact → entry rounds; `bin` sorts
  active rays by the metric's three-way depth branch (scheduling-only on
  CPU, the ~2.3x divergence lever on device).
- **Certified**: wavefront_raymap ≡ render_raymap bit-identically on RK4 /
  starved / dopri budgets × sweep 1/7/64 × bin on/off × ambient + situated
  cameras; pinned in test/ (289 cold green); physics_diff exactly 0.0.
- **Surprise**: the staged CPU driver runs 0.59–0.68x the recursive
  renderer's time (96×72 cube, 16 threads) — render_raymap threads over
  rows and ray cost is row-uneven, the compacted active list
  self-load-balances, and the boxes are gone. Attribution plausible, not
  isolated; recorded in measurements.md 2026-07-14d.
- **Step-5 remainder**: the device tracer kernel itself — sweep_ray with
  settle/transitions/to_ambient in-kernel on the Adapt-ed throat
  (gpu_christoffel.jl scaffolding promotes to gpu/ then), host entry
  stage feeding device sweeps; after it traces, the register-pressure
  lever (hand-rolled derivatives in production, AD stays the reference
  oracle) and the Jacobi-field κ endgame.

# 2026-07-14, fourth session part 2 — the device tracer kernel runs; the price is named

- **Kaarel's checkpoint**: "device tracer kernel" defined — a CUDA kernel
  whose per-thread body is sweep_ray: one thread owns one ray and marches
  it (settled RK4/dopri attempts, chart hops, half transitions, to_ambient
  exit), replacing exactly one line of run_wavefront! (the Threads.@threads
  sweep) with a @cuda launch; entry solves and compaction stay host-side.
  Proceeded on that basis.
- **Landing**: run_wavefront!'s sweep stage became a swappable callable
  (threaded_sweep! the default; the 14d bit-identity tests pin that this
  changed nothing — 289 cold, physics_diff 0.0). gpu/jupitergpu.jl is the
  promoted gpu module the plan foresaw: PackedTable/adapt/device_throat
  moved out of gpu_christoffel.jl (which re-verifies: 11x one CPU thread
  F32 at 20k points, parallelism-consistent with 14b's 14.3x at 200k),
  plus kernel_sweep + DeviceSweep (gather → launch → scatter, kernel
  seconds accumulated) + device_raymap.
- **Correct on-card** (192×144 cube, 27,648 rays): 0 side flips and 0
  res-mismatches in all three comparisons; F64 device vs CPU has 85% of
  rays bit-identical through whole traces, the rest transcendental-
  intrinsic ulps (q99 3.1e-15 rad); F32 device vs CPU F32 q99 1.5e-6; F32
  vs F64 truth mirrors 14c. The oracle chain reaches the card: device runs
  the same sweep_ray that's bit-identical to the recursive tracer that
  physics_diff certifies against reference.
- **Slow, and diagnosably so**: naive device F32 8.35s best vs 1.09s CPU
  wavefront F32 (16 threads); kernel-only ≈ wall (host stages + transfers
  negligible — the wavefront split is structurally right); F64 only ~2x
  F32 on a 1:64-FP64 card ⇒ spill-bound (255 regs, 42.5K/68.9K local
  bytes/thread), and 27.6k shrinking rays can't hide local latency.
  Binning ~15% at sweep=64. Consistent with 14b's break-even projection
  for bare christoffel at 200k-point parallelism. Full data
  measurements.md 2026-07-14e.
- **Consequence for the plan**: step-5 structure is complete. GPU
  throughput is gated on the hand-rolled-derivative register lever
  (measured now, not hypothesized) and parallelism supply (frame batches
  — the fly-through case provides them naturally); persistent-thread
  scheduling is the third lever.
- **Correction from kaarel's question** (does "more rays" include plain
  resolution? yes): the 255-regs→1-block/SM saturation arithmetic predicted
  the card full at ~14k rays; measured scaling refutes it — device F32
  per-ray 848/285/109 µs at 7k/28k/111k rays, i.e. 2.8x behind the CPU at
  384×288 *and still improving* (wave quantization + compaction-shrunk
  tails idle SMs far past nominal fill). Starvation is a real,
  resolution-curable half of the gap; the other half is per-thread spill.
  The two GPU throughput gates multiply: rays-in-flight (resolution or
  frame batches — the flat pool takes a frame id trivially) × the
  hand-rolled-derivative register lever. Addendum in measurements.md 14e.
