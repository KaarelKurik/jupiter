# Session 2026-08-01 — deep fusion: the Gauss collapse, the 8-lane tower, and an honest neutral

Numbers and why-chains in measurements.md 2026-08-01 + 2026-08-01b. Two jj
changes: the opener (Gauss identity in reference) and the fusion landing.

- **Kaarel's opening question** — what "a second jet algebra" means for
  deep fusion, and whether fully opaque fusion (semantics fixed by
  reference, jet logic implicit) would do — reshaped the design twice.
  First, working the EL form through the collar structure before choosing:
  with g = JᵀJ, the contraction collapses past the plan's sketch to the
  **Gauss formula** w = Jᵀ(D_vD_v c) (the ∂J·J terms cancel through
  ∂_i e_u = ∂_u e_i). Kaarel recognized it from prior work of his (a
  star-shaped-mesh application). Probe-verified against reference AD at
  6e-15 (Gauss, outer), 1.4e-13 (EL restatement, everywhere), 7e-15
  (blend decomposition); landed as `Reference.pullback_accel` + 3 tests
  (the opener change). Consequence: 8 tower lanes (full Jet2 + two
  doubly-contracted thirds), the sym-matrix gradient layer leaves the
  geodesic path, the inner arm needs no thirds at all (its w collapses to
  cs·(∂s)ᵀ(D_wD_w s) — the cylinder's constant d-velocity falls out).
- **The design answer**: perf-wise algebra-vs-opaque is a wash (@inline
  structs SROA to the same SSA); the axis is where derivation errors hide.
  So: a *private* 8-lane algebra (DJet) as correctness scaffolding for the
  tower — every op certified against contracting the Jet3 op, order-2
  lanes exactly equal by op-for-op discipline — and genuinely opaque
  bespoke assembly above it (normal chain, three arms), per kaarel's
  instinct. The h-lane chain rules (dleibniz/ddiv/dcompose1, dre/dim_jet,
  the directional Wirtinger bridge) were each derived twice
  independently; the bridge's key fact: polynomial thirds enter only
  through (Hx, Hy) = D_ΩD_Ω(∇p), Ω = ζ′·ŵ — contracted pair through the
  pushforward, free index through the jacobian.
- **Landed and certified**: DJet + surface_djet + fused geodesic_accel
  (gradient body retained as oracle; christoffel/transport keep Jet3);
  kernel_sweep threads env (seam fidelity + what made the A/B
  interleavable in one warm process). 782 cold green; physics_diff 0

- **Permission saga** (recorded for next time): GPU counters are

  the *current boot only* (devtmpfs), so kaarel's script-then-reboot
  undid itself; re-running post-boot worked. Persistent fix (untested):
  the modprobe regkey + `mkinitcpio -P`. Watchdog: SASS-patching passes

  model. Round 2's 33 ms floor seen directly (29 blocks, DRAM 6%); SMs
