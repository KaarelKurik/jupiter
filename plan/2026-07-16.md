- **Shape of the derivation** (src/jets.jl, surface_jet in chart.jl,
  assembly in geodesic.jl):
  - the wedge chain is holomorphic in z = u+iv (rotation ∘ z^p ∘ affine;
    next-corner is w ↦ i + (−i)w), so its order-3 jet is 4 complex numbers
    with 1D chain rules — z^p via φ⁽ᵏ⁺¹⁾ = (p−k)φ⁽ᵏ⁾/z on top of
    fake_complex_pow's value; Cauchy–Riemann converts to real (u,v) partials
    at the seam to real code;
  - poly ∘ holomorphic composes in Wirtinger form (∂z only through w, ∂z̄
    only through w̄): the bivariate Faà di Bruno factorizes into two 1D
    chains, five D-terms since D_ab = conj(D_ba) for real output; the ten
    poly partials come from one table read (derivative-carrying double
    Horner);
  - blends are scalar exp(−1/x) chains, gated on b == 0 so F32 near-seam
    evaluation can't produce 0·Inf;
  - depth never needs a jet: collar columns are linear in d, inner metric
    d-free, depth blend a 1D chain (step-4's "depth factors out exactly",
    cashed in); n̂ is an order-2 jet; the Γ contraction is unchanged.
  - value lanes reproduce the plain evaluators op-for-op — surface_jet's
    value is bit-identical to surface(), so any oracle mismatch during
    development had to be a derivative lane. There were none: the probe
    battery passed on first run.
